Frequencies of three clades of interest in the squirrel network samples.

For each sampling run, the count is how many of the input networks contain the
split "clade | rest" -- i.e. have that clade as a hardwired cluster of the
network (computed with PhyloNetworks). For the unrooted runs the networks carry
an arbitrary rooting, so a split is counted whenever either side appears as a
cluster, which makes the count rooting-invariant.
